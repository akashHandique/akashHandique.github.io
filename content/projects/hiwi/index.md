---
title: Sample collection at River Danube, Bavaria, Germany
date: 2022-10-01


---

As part of a collaborative project with MIT, USA, I joined the University of Stuttgart team on a field trip to the Danube River in Bavaria, Germany, in 2022. The objective was to collect river sediment and water samples to assess river colmation and associated challenges in the region. We employed vertical sampling techniques and used liquid nitrogen to preserve sample integrity and minimise changes in sample properties during transport. The collected samples were subsequently processed and analysed in the laboratory.
