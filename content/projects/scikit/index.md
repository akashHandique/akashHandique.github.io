---
title: 6-Months DLR, Stuttgart research visit
date: 2023-10-26


---
I completed a six-month research visit to the German Aerospace Center (DLR) in Stuttgart, Germany, based within DLR's Energy Systems Analysis department, to co-develop the REMix-PIC energy transition model for Pacific Island countries. During the visit, I worked closely with DLR researchers on open-source energy systems modelling practices and on structuring and developing energy systems datasets for regional analysis, an exchange that directly shaped the methodology later published as the EDRS-PIC data descriptor. I also presented this work to the department in December 2025, and the collaboration established during the visit has continued beyond it, contributing to an ongoing joint publication on Pacific Island renewable electricity pathways
